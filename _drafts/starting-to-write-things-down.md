I have been building things for two years without writing any of it down. The problem with
that is not the writing, it is that I keep solving the same problem twice and only
noticing the second time.

So this page is the fix. Short posts, written when I learn something worth keeping.

## What goes here

- **Job search data.** I run a job board that pulls postings directly from the systems
  companies actually use, which means I end up with numbers nobody publishes.
- **Automation that held up.** The workflows that survived contact with real data, and the
  ones that did not.
- **Build notes.** Decisions I made, what they cost, and what I would do differently.

Not going here: hot takes, tool roundups, or anything I have not actually run.

## How this works

Posts are markdown files in this site's repository. I write one, push it, and GitHub builds
the page. There is no editor, no dashboard, and no account. If you would rather not check
back, the [RSS feed](/feed.xml) has everything.

That is the whole setup. Now the hard part, which is having something to say.
